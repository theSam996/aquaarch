3D Foot Scan

Record a video of your feet for precise 3D analysis and custom insole creation.

Recording Instructions

1

Position Your Feet

Place your feet flat on the floor, shoulder-width apart. Stand naturally without leaning.

2

Camera Position

Position your camera at a 45-degree angle from the side, about 2-3 feet away.

3

Lighting

Ensure good lighting. Avoid shadows on your feet. Natural light works best.

4

Recording

Record for 10-15 seconds. Slowly rotate your feet if possible for better analysis.
